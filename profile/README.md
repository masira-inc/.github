# Masira

**The intelligence layer between what shoppers mean and what a brand has in stock.**

Masira reads a brand's product catalog, standardizes its attributes, and adds the details shoppers ask about in plain language: occasion, style, material, care. The result works in two places.

- **On the brand's storefront:** a search API that answers natural language queries, such as "something for a dinner date in Miami, it's hot, under $300", with products that are in stock.
- **Across AI shopping agents:** product feeds in the formats Google Merchant Center, Microsoft Merchant Center, OpenAI and Perplexity read, and a recurring measurement of how often assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity recommend the brand.

Masira works across fashion, beauty, home, consumer electronics and travel gear.

## How brands connect

- Shopify, WooCommerce or a CSV file
- A REST API and an MCP server for teams that build on their own stack
